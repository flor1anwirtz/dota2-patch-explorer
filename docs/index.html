Data and methods

Sample coverage and definitionsSampling dates, match eligibility and how to read the rates.

Which matches qualify?

Each sample includes ranked matches lasting at least 20 minutes. The duration groups use final game time. A 30-minute game belongs to the middle group, and a 40-minute game belongs to the longest group.

How are rates calculated?

Radiant win rate is the share of matches Radiant won. The side-to-side gap is the absolute difference between Radiant’s and Dire’s win rates. The patch change subtracts the first patch’s Radiant rate from the second’s, in percentage points.

How are ranks grouped?

The match’s average medal places it in one of three groups. Herald through Crusader, Archon through Ancient, and Divine with Immortal form the low, middle and high groups.

What can these samples tell us?

These rates describe the sampled matches. The patches can cover different dates, hours and player populations. Duration is known only after the game ends.
